# Weekly Meeting Workflow

### Before the Meeting

**Meeting Lead**
- Check current GitHub Issues
- Review important Telegram discussions
- Prepare the agenda

**Note Taker**
- Create a new meeting note
- Get the agenda from the Meeting Lead

---

### During the Meeting

**Meeting Lead**
- Review completed and ongoing work
- Assign tasks

**Everyone**
- Give a short progress update
- Mention problems or blockers
- Create / update their Issues

**Note Taker**
- Record important decisions and action items

---

### After the Meeting

**Meeting Lead**
- Assign next week's Meeting Lead and Note Taker

**Note Taker**
- Finalize the meeting notes on GitHub
